# SOGIE & GAD Interview Report

**Interviewee:** Hon. Virginia G. Ong
**Age:** 48
**Position:** Sangguniang Bayan (SB) Member, Sindangan, Zamboanga del Norte
**Length of Service:** 1st term

## Interview Responses
* **GAD Policies Awareness:** 
	* Guided by CEDAW and the Magna Carta of Women (RA 9710), ensuring the mandatory 5% GAD budget allocation
	> Yes. Internationally, there is the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (CEDAW). Locally, our municipality is strictly guided by the Magna Carta of Women (Republic Act No. 9710) and the mandatory 5% GAD budget allocation for local government units.
* **Proposed GAD Ordinances:** 
	* Co-sponsored resolutions for the annual GAD Plan (funding maternal health, VAWC desks, and local livelihood).
	> As part of the municipal council, I regularly co-sponsor the resolutions approving our annual GAD Plan and Budget. This ensures our local funds are explicitly directed toward maternal health services, the maintenance of VAWC (Violence Against Women and their Children) desks across our barangays, and local livelihood training.
* **Spearheaded Programs:** 
	* Backed National Women's Month initiatives and livelihood seminars for mothers in coastal/agricultural communities.
	> I actively support our local National Women's Month initiatives every March and help spearhead community-level livelihood seminars. These activities aim to provide financial independence and skills training for mothers in our coastal and agricultural communities here in Sindangan.
* **SOGIE Discrimination Cases:** 
	* Addressed subtle community discrimination by mediating issues alongside local barangay captains.
	> While Sindanganons are naturally welcoming, subtle discrimination occasionally happens in workplaces or community spaces. When informal complaints reach my office, I coordinate with the respective barangay captains to mediate the issue and ensure the individuals involved are treated with dignity and fairness.
* **Future SOGIE Ordinance:** 
	* Planning to draft a local Anti-Discrimination Ordinance penalizing SOGIE-based prejudice in employment, education, and municipal services.
	> Yes. Drafting a comprehensive local Anti-Discrimination Ordinance is a goal of mine. The basic content would prohibit and penalize discrimination based on sexual orientation, gender identity, and expression (SOGIE) in local employment, educational institutions, and the delivery of municipal public services.
* **Future SOGIE Programs:** 
	* Proposing SOGIE sensitivity and awareness seminars specifically for municipal frontliners, local police, and barangay officials.
	>Absolutely. I plan to propose SOGIE sensitivity and awareness seminars specifically tailored for our municipal frontliners, local police, and barangay officials. This will ensure that when LGBTQ+ constituents seek public assistance, they are met with understanding rather than prejudice.
